# Slice 04 — Refunds and credit settlement

## Goal

Settle unallocated credit that a payment holds. A refund returns money to the debtor without
changing the amount already allocated to installments.

## Scope boundary

This slice refunds only unallocated credit.

It does not reverse or charge back money that was already allocated to an installment. A reversal
would reopen the plan and is a separate business process.

## Endpoints

```text
POST /api/claims/{claimNumber}/payment-plan/payments/{paymentId}/refunds
GET  /api/claims/{claimNumber}/payment-plan/refunds
```

Recording returns `201 Created` with the refund, the updated payment and the updated plan.
History returns refunds ordered by `refundedOn`, then internal id.

## Data model

### `Refund`

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `refundId` | Public UUID, generated by the server. |
| `payment` | The payment that holds the credit. |
| `amount` | Positive, scale 2, at most the payment's remaining unallocated credit. |
| `refundedOn` | Required, not in the future relative to the Berlin clock. |
| `recordedAt` | Server timestamp. |
| `reference` | Optional, trimmed, maximum 255 characters, unique per payment when present. |

`Payment.refunds` and `PaymentPlan.refunds` are ordered. The plan-level refund history is the
union of refunds for all payments of the plan.

## Money after a refund

```text
payment.allocatedAmount   = sum(allocation.amount)          (unchanged)
payment.refundedAmount    = sum(refund.amount)
payment.unallocatedAmount = payment.amount - allocatedAmount - refundedAmount

plan.amountPaid           = sum(installment.amountPaid)      (unchanged)
plan.outstandingBalance   = sum(installment outstanding)     (unchanged)
plan.creditAmount         = sum(payment.unallocatedAmount)
plan.refundedAmount       = sum(refund.amount)
```

Example:

```text
Payment 500
  allocated: 300
  credit:    200

Refund 50
  allocated: 300
  refunded:  50
  credit:    150
```

A second refund of `150` exhausts the credit. A further refund returns `409`.

The gross and currently held view:

```text
grossReceived  = amountPaid + creditAmount + refundedAmount
currentlyHeld  = amountPaid + creditAmount
```

## Rules

- A refund can target only unallocated credit.
- The amount must not exceed the payment's remaining `unallocatedAmount`.
- Partial refunds are allowed.
- Several refunds per payment are allowed until the credit is exhausted.
- A payment with no credit cannot be refunded.
- A refund does not change installment paid amounts or the outstanding balance.
- A refund does not reopen a completed plan. It stays `COMPLETED`.
- `refundedOn` is required and must not be in the future.
- `reference` is optional and unique per payment.
- The same pessimistic claim lock used by payments also serialises refunds.
- The public refund id is a UUID.

## Errors

| Status | Case |
| --- | --- |
| `201` | Refund recorded. |
| `400` | Missing body, null or non-positive amount, amount needing rounding, missing or future `refundedOn`, invalid claim number, malformed JSON. |
| `404` | Unknown claim, no plan, unknown payment, or a payment from another plan. |
| `409` | Amount above available credit, duplicate reference, lock conflict or data conflict. |

## Concurrency

Refunds use the same `PESSIMISTIC_WRITE` claim lock as payments. Payments and refunds for the same
claim serialise.

A two-thread test proves that two refunds cannot spend the same credit twice: exactly one
succeeds, the other gets an insufficient-credit conflict, and the final credit and refunded
amounts are correct.

## Assumptions and rules

- Refunds are per payment, because the credit belongs to the payment that created it.
- A refund cannot move credit to another claim or another payment.
- A refund is not a payment reversal.
- The plan status is not changed by a refund. `COMPLETED` remains terminal in this model.
- No idempotent replay. A reference is a duplicate guard, not a replay key.
- Refund history is not paginated.
- No accounting export, tax handling or settlement file.

## Verification

Coverage includes:

- refund factory validation and reference normalization;
- partial refunds and multiple refunds;
- exact exhaustion of credit;
- over-refund and no-credit rejection;
- unchanged allocation and installment paid amounts;
- plan credit net of refunds;
- repository cascade, duplicate reference and ordered history;
- service success and error cases;
- web recording, history and `ProblemDetail` errors;
- full create → overpay → refund → read flow;
- two-thread refund concurrency.

See [Build, test and CI](00-build-test-and-ci.md).

## Out of scope

- Reversing or charging back an allocated payment.
- Reopening a completed plan.
- Moving credit between claims or payments.
- Refund idempotency replay.
- History pagination.
- Accounting or export formats.
